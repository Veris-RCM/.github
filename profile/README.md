# Veris RCM

**Clearer revenue operations for independent practices.**

We’re building Veris to connect the work behind professional claims—from coverage and claim readiness to follow-up and reconciliation.

Our initial focus is independent practices using Healthie. Our goal is straightforward: less routine administration, clearer responsibilities, and better visibility into what needs attention.

## What we’re building

- **Claim readiness:** Bring documentation, provider requirements, and coverage evidence into one workflow.
- **Claims and follow-up:** Track submissions, acknowledgements, corrections, and denials with clear next steps.
- **Financial reconciliation:** Connect remittances to claims and make discrepancies visible.
- **Practice coordination:** Keep missing information, exceptions, and decisions connected to the people responsible.

## How we build

We believe useful automation needs clear boundaries and evidence behind its actions.

Our engineering priorities include explicit authorization, tenant isolation, auditable workflows, accessible interfaces, and thorough testing. Demonstrations and tests use synthetic data.

Our stack includes TypeScript, React, TanStack, PostgreSQL, Google Cloud, and Pulumi.

## Follow our progress

Veris is in active development. We’re shaping the product through conversations with practices and carefully scoped demonstrations.
